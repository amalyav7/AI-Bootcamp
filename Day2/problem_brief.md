Prompt: "I am a student learning about AI. Please give me 3 small AI app ideas that solve real-world problems with the problem, user, and core AI feature."



Problem Statement: People can often forget to take their medication or accidentally take it twice. 



Target User: People who take medications or administer them, such as patients and caregivers.



Why Current Solutions Fall Short: Most apps currently have fixed alarms and don't adjust to the user's schedule changes or confirm whether the medicine was taken.



My App Will...: Remind users when to take their medication and verify it was taken while adjusting to the user's schedule.

Scope for 10 Days: Create an app that reminds users when to take medications, verifies they were taken, and accesses the user's calendar to check their schedule.
